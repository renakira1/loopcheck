About the loop

The full clip is decoded before playback. Cached frames follow a continuous audio clock; audio loops in one buffer, without restarting a media player. Compare the original with an end-to-start dissolve and audio crossfade. Repairs shorten the clip by the overlap length. Optional quiet-edge removal cuts picture and audio together; it may remove intentional silence. Clips must be 30 seconds or shorter and fit a 128 MB decoded cache. Preview resolution may be reduced. Downloads re-encode at the original resolution (rounded up to even dimensions), with a base cadence up to 60 fps and extra dissolve frames, using MP4 or WebM according to browser encoding support. Source metadata and extra tracks are not retained. A dissolve may show ghosting; missing sound cannot be reconstructed. Exported codec padding or another player can still affect looping. Display refresh, device load, and silence already encoded in the file can still affect your test. Playback pauses when this page is hidden.
